General

Visuals

mcMMO integration

Active only when the mcMMO plugin is installed. Crops grant Herbalism XP and roll Herbalism double drops; foods with a diet benefit from Farmer's/Fisherman's Diet.

Crop highlight (look-at outline)

Insanity

The 0-100 psychological score that drives hallucination effects.

Quality tiers

Thresholds are the minimum prep score for each tier (must increase, 0-1). Multipliers scale effect duration per tier.

Ruined batch item

Stations

Placed crafting devices. A process handled by any station is crafted at that station's menu instead of the grid. oven_top stations install on a furnace, smoker, blast furnace, campfire, or soul campfire. A block makes it a plate station (rendered as that block with embedded heads). An optional recipe crafts the station's head item in a vanilla grid.

Vanilla food spoilage

Minutes before plain vanilla foods spoil after entering an inventory. 0 = never spoils.

Biome groups

Named, composable lists of biome keys (a member may be another group).

Consumable groups

Named groups of materials / consumable ids / other groups, usable as recipe tags.

Vanilla crops

Vanilla crops grow at full speed in their natural biomes and at the outside-rate elsewhere. Biomes may be keys or biome-group names.
