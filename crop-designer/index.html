for carrot-style crops where produce is replanted

0 = this crop's seed will never drop from grass/ferns/bushes, in any biome.

Seeds drop from grass and small plants in the crop's natural biomes. grass_seeds.drop_chance controls how often any seed drops. This weight chooses which seed drops. A weight of 0.3 is three times as likely as 0.1. Choose "consumable is seed" when the harvest and seed are the same item, like a carrot.

Growth playback

Playback follows the configured crop time and biome multiplier.

Full speed inside natural biomes, otherwise growth is multiplied by this rate. Leaving this empty is full speed everywhere.

Sounds and particles played crop-wide. on_punch fires when the crop is punched/broken, on_harvest when a ripe crop is harvested, and ambient repeatedly while it grows. Each growth stage can override these (see a stage's "stage effects").
